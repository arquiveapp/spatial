# Local lab and rights tooling

Read [tooling rules](../scripts/AGENTS.md). Lab pages are experiments outside npm
artifacts. No automatic camera recording, uploads or device support promotion.
Export measurements only on an explicit download or authorized lab-send button. The optional
phone-testing tunnel and local result receiver follow [mobile lab](../docs/mobile-lab.md);
never send camera media or expose private fixture paths/results over HTTP. Keep all local
fixtures, access links and received reports out of Git/npm. Never infer physical results
from mocks, desktop runs or synthetic replay. Stop resources on page hide.
