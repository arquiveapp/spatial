# Asset provenance

No third-party models, textures, datasets or camera recordings are committed or distributed in npm.
The lab cube and synthetic textures are generated by original MIT source.

The user authorized existing apartment GLBs from their ARchive web work for local phone tests.
Those originals are served read-only through `.local/lab-config.json` paths with exact SHA-256
identity. No model is copied into this repository; private paths, access links and diagnostic
reports remain in ignored `.local/`. This authorization is for testing, not redistribution under
Spatial's MIT licence. Do not add these GLBs/textures to Git, npm or other public hosting.
